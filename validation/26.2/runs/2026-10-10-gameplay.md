and off; ghast skills enter charging and then release; warden skills start the real

default flying speed restored. Bat night vision appears, stops refreshing after
unmorph, remains briefly for its existing duration, then expires within 300 ticks.

Final clients and servers stop normally with exit code 0 and empty `logIssues`.
Offline auth/Realms, missing narrator and OpenAL-device failures are recorded under
`environmentErrors`; sound playback is not validated. Earlier failed fixtures remain
on disk and are not counted: a reset scheduled after skill activation overwrote
cooldown, and a night-vision assertion ran before the configured effect expired.

`../FeatherMorph/validation/26.2/runs/2026-10-10-gameplay.md` relative to this
repository root. It covers ten effective active skill types and all 27 registered
passive types, while these client checks cover representative synchronization.

listener-test events, cross-region targets and NeoForge are not covered.
